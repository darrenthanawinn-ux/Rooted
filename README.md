# Rooted 🌱
### A hyper-local, cash-free skill & resource barter network

Rooted lets students trade what they know for what they need — tutoring for
language practice, a calculator for lab notes, guitar lessons for a
home-cooked meal — with an AI Matchmaker that finds your best trade partner
nearby, and a shared Community Garden that grows a little greener with every
completed trade.

## What's inside

- **`index.html`** — the entire app. One self-contained file: no build step,
  no external dependencies, no internet connection required. Just open it in
  any modern browser.

## How to run it

1. Unzip this folder.
2. Double-click `index.html` (or open it from your browser with `File → Open`).
3. That's it — it's already populated with mock offers, requests, and a
   grown-in garden so it looks alive from the first second.

No installs, no servers, no API keys. It will run identically on a laptop
with no wifi, which makes it safe for a live demo.

## Features

### 🧺 The Board
A live-filtering feed of Offers and Requests across four categories —
Skills, Study Gear, Tutoring, and Creative/Tech — with a search bar and
category chips so you can instantly test finding a match. Pre-populated
with 16 realistic posts from 11 different community members.

### 💡 AI Matchmaker
A dedicated tab where you describe what you can offer and what you need.
A simulated smart-matching pass "thinks" for a moment, then surfaces a
match with a compatibility score and a one-tap way to start the trade —
the core "wow" moment of the demo.

### 🌿 The Community Garden
Every completed trade — from the Board or the Matchmaker — plants a new
flower, tree, or seedling in a shared garden canvas, with live stats for
trades completed, plants grown, and people connected, plus a running log
of who traded what with whom.

### 🤝 Trade Negotiation Flow
Clicking "Propose Trade" opens a clean agreement modal with a mock chat
exchange. Accepting triggers a satisfying checkmark animation, a success
toast, and immediate garden growth — declining just closes the modal, no
harm done.

### 🎨 Look & feel
Soft watercolor-and-parchment aesthetic: cream, sage, dusty rose, and
warm amber, rounded serif headers, gentle floating dust motes in the
background, and organic micro-interactions throughout. Fully responsive
from desktop down to mobile.

## Customizing

Everything — mock users, posts, garden log, category labels, color
palette — lives in plain JavaScript objects and CSS variables near the
top of `index.html`, so it's easy to swap in your own data before a demo
or presentation.
